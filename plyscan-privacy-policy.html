PlyScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

PlyScan ("the app") is a chess app made by Josue Miguel Vallo ("I", "me"). It reads a photo of a chessboard, lets you fix the position, and analyses it. You can scan a few photos for free, and a one-time purchase unlocks the rest. This page explains what the app does with information.

The short version

  • Your photos are read on your phone. They are not uploaded to me or to anyone else.
  • There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking in the app.
  • I do not run a server for the app, and the app does not send me any information about you.
  • The one-time unlock is paid through Google Play. I never receive your card or payment details.
  • The app connects to the internet only for the four things listed under "When the app uses the internet" below.

Photos and the camera

When you tap Take photo, the app asks for permission to use the camera. When you tap Choose photo, you pick a picture with your phone's own picker. The photo is analysed by the scanner that is built into the app, on your phone, and is not sent anywhere.

The app saves a photo to your gallery only when you tap Save photo under it. The copy goes into an album called PlyScan on your phone, and you can delete it like any other photo. The app never saves photos by itself.

Free scans and the one-time unlock

The app counts how many photos you have scanned and, after the free scans are used, asks you to unlock it with a one-time purchase (not a subscription). The count, and a yes or no for "this phone has the unlock", are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not sent to me.

The purchase is handled by Google Play. When you open the unlock screen, tap Unlock or Restore purchase, or open the app, PlyScan asks Google Play on your phone whether your Google account has bought the unlock and which price to show. The payment itself is between you and Google; see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms. Google may show me sales information about purchases, such as an order number, the date, the amount and the country, as it does for every app developer on Google Play. I do not receive your card or account details.

Information kept on your phone

To work properly the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your phone:

  • the corrections you make to a scanned photo, so the same photo is read the same way next time (the app can forget them with the "Forget my corrections" button);
  • your choices for board colours, piece style and engine;
  • how many free scans you have used, and whether this phone has the unlock;
  • if you choose to download it, the Stockfish engine files.

This stays on your phone. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The purchase itself is kept by Google on your Google account, so after a reinstall you can get the unlock back with Restore purchase.

When the app uses the internet

  • Fonts. When the app opens it may load its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Like any website or app that does this, Google receives your IP address and basic request details. See Google's Privacy Policy. If the fonts cannot be loaded, the app uses your phone's own fonts.
  • Google Play (the unlock). The check and the purchase described above go through the Google Play app on your phone.
  • Open on Lichess / Open on Chess.com. Only when you tap one of these buttons, the app opens that website in your browser with the current chess position in the web address. Those sites then handle your visit under their own policies: Lichess and Chess.com.
  • Download Stockfish. Only when you tap Download Stockfish, the app downloads the Stockfish chess engine files from a public software host (unpkg.com, or cdn.jsdelivr.net if the first one fails). That host sees your IP address and the request, as with any download.

The app does not send your photos, positions or settings to any server.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to take a photo of your board when you tap Take photo.
  • Internet: for the uses above.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save a photo into your gallery when you tap Save photo.
  • Google Play billing: to offer the one-time unlock through Google Play.

Advertising, analytics, accounts and selling data

The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no account or sign-in, and it does not sell or share personal information.

Children

The app is a general chess tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect any from children.

Your choices

You can use the app without tapping any of the buttons that go online. You can clear the app's storage or uninstall the app at any time to remove everything it keeps on your phone. Questions about a purchase or a refund are handled by Google Play.

Open-source software

The app includes open-source software and piece-set artwork. Their licences and credits are listed inside the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page and the date at the top.
