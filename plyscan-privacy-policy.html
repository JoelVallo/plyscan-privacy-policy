Privacy Policy for PlyScan

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

PlyScan ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Chess Board Images: The primary function of PlyScan is to analyze photographs of chess boards that you capture using your device's camera.
  • Device Identifiers: We may collect device identifiers for analytics and error reporting purposes.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Analytics: We collect information about how you interact with the application, including features used and frequency of use.
  • Crash Reports: If the application crashes or encounters errors, we collect diagnostic information to improve stability.
  • Device Information: We collect information about your device type, operating system version, and app version.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve the core chess board scanning functionality
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues and errors
  • To analyze usage patterns and enhance user experience
  • To develop new features and improvements
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Camera and Photo Access

PlyScan requires access to your device's camera to capture chess board images. You have complete control over this permission and can revoke camera access at any time through your device's settings. The application will not function without camera access.

Chess board images are processed locally on your device and are not stored, transmitted, or retained by PlyScan unless you explicitly choose to save them to your device's photo library.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. You can delete your usage data by uninstalling the application. Chess board images are not retained on our servers.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Third-Party Services

PlyScan may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (such as Firebase or similar services). These third parties have their own privacy policies governing the data they collect. We do not control how these third parties use your data, and you should review their privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

PlyScan is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access: You may request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your data by uninstalling the application or contacting us.
  • Opt-Out: You can disable analytics and crash reporting through the application settings.
  • Permission Control: You can revoke camera permissions through your device's settings at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
